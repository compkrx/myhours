# Mapping a private master plan to the app

The public app implements structures and decision rules. Its source contains no personal grade ledger, identity, school, training schedule or private project history. Personal plan records belong in an external private backup/import file and are loaded only into the local workspace after review.

| Plan concept                               | App location                                                         | Trust boundary                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Current reality and grade recovery         | School: reported snapshot, assignments, actual assessment points     | Historical percentages have an as-of date and source; verify them before treating them as current |
| Daily priority decision                    | Today / Inbox / Calendar                                             | Real deadlines and dependencies outrank broad monthly themes                                      |
| Normal / training / heavy / low-energy day | Calendar: actual fixed events plus conservative draft or minimum day | No training sessions or future school dates are inferred                                          |
| Weekly rhythm and Sunday review            | Weekly review, routines, study sessions                              | Optional themes never become automatic assignments                                                |
| Learning methods                           | Learning: focus, practice, mistake log, writing and evidence         | Minutes and confidence do not imply mastery                                                       |
| Four-year course and career decisions      | Long horizon / Explore                                               | Checkpoints remain proposed; prerequisites, dates and eligibility require sources                 |
| Monthly checkpoints                        | Long horizon: year filter and month-sorted timeline                  | Turning one into a task opens a draft, without inventing a deadline                               |
| Daily five-field check-in                  | Long horizon: choose date and open daily log                         | Due work, academic action, fixed activities, result and next step are entered by the user         |
| One major build                            | Projects                                                             | Additional builds are queued until the active one is paused or complete                           |
| Sports, rest and free time                 | Lifestyle / Calendar / Settings                                      | Real commitments, buffers, sleep opportunity and reserved time constrain optional work            |
| Money and business learning                | Money / Projects                                                     | Manual actual amounts; no predicted income, bank connections or trades                            |
| Read-only discussion with ChatGPT          | Settings: selected snapshot                                          | Preview, redaction and separate authorization; no automatic awareness                             |

Exact future graduation dates, school calendars, policy and eligibility remain unknown unless freshly verified. A proposed planning endpoint is not a ceremony date. Imported notes describe assumptions; no years-long schedule is silently committed.
